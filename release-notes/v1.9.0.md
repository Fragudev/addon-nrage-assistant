# Nrage Assistant — Announcements, modules and rank mapping (v1.9.0)

A guild announcements board, a way to turn off the parts of the addon you do not use, roles that survive a rank
rename, and PvP missions that count better and can be configured in more ways.

## ✨ New

### Announcements
- **Announcements board** in the Guild group: every member reads the guild's announcements, pinned ones first
- **Popup** when a new announcement arrives, if the author chose to send it; it waits until you leave combat, and at
  login you get the ones you missed
- **Announcements editor** in the Admin group, for the Emperador and the Comandante only: title, text, send popup or
  not, pin, expiry, edit, delete and "send popup again"
- The newest pinned announcement also shows on Home, with an unread count in the sidebar

### Modules
- New **Modules** tab: turn Professions, Raid, Loot, PvP, Intel, Groups, Layers, Guild map, Blacklist and
  Announcements on or off **for yourself only**
- A module that is off hides its tabs and stops its alerts and popups; its data keeps arriving, so nothing is lost
  when you turn it back on
- A module turned on after login asks for a reload, with a button

### Rank mapping
- New **Ranks** tab in the Admin group: each guild rank is tied to an addon role **by its position**, so renaming a
  rank in the game no longer changes anyone's permissions
- The Emperador and the Comandante edit it; a Comandante cannot change their own rank, and the Emperador can always
  undo a Comandante's change
- A preview shows who changes role before saving, and a banner warns when ranks were renamed, added or removed
- Until someone saves a mapping, roles keep coming from the rank names, exactly as before

## 🎨 Changes

### PvP missions
- Kills count **even before the season reaches you**, and a season change no longer wipes your log
- Two kills of the same player within a few seconds now both count
- Battleground wins are detected more reliably, and losses are recorded too
- Enemy classes and races follow your faction
- New **Battlegrounds played** goal, and a battleground filter for wins
- New mission options: victim classes and races, a per-victim daily limit, a minimum of guildmates in your group, a
  time window, a maximum of completions and auto-claim, plus templates and a summary line in the mission form
- Mission cards explain their rules in plain words and say when a mission is **ready to claim**
- Approvers get a **Progress** view of who is close to each mission, and pending claims are re-sent when an approver
  comes online

## ⚠️ Good to know

- **Update everyone:** members on an older version ignore the new mission options and do not see "Battlegrounds
  played" missions
- Killing blows are not counted yet: they wait for in-game checks of the combat log
- None of these changes has been checked in the live game yet; the rank mapping in particular assumes the order of
  the guild ranks matches what the roster reports
