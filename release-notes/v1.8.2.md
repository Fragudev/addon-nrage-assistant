# Nrage Assistant — New guild ranks and Intel fixes (v1.8.2)

The addon follows the guild's new rank names, Intel respects your settings everywhere, and the maps and the Intel
window do less work every second.

## 🔧 Changes

### Guild ranks
- Roles now come from the new rank names: **Emperador** (guild master), **Comandante** (co-guild master),
  **Oficial**, **Raid Lider** (also "Raid Líder"), **Raider**, **Soldado** and **Alter**
- The old names (GM, CoGM, RL, Roster, Miembro) are no longer recognised: a member whose rank still has an old name
  counts as Alter until the rank is renamed in the game
- The guild master is still recognised by holding the top rank, whatever it is called

## 🐛 Fixes

### Intel
- Nothing is recorded where detection is off (Intel disabled, dungeons, excluded zones, battlegrounds or arenas with
  that setting off): the minimap tooltip, deaths and honour kills now follow the same rule as the nameplates
- A damaged saved enemy record no longer stops the detection of the enemies around you

## ⚡ Performance

- Guild and enemy map pins are only drawn again when something on them changed
- The Intel window does less work on each refresh
- Less work when a large enemy list is full, and for alerts and the Groups counter

## ⚠️ Good to know

- **Rename the ranks in the game** before or right after updating, or members will lose their permissions
- Wins and losses in places where detection is off are no longer counted
- These changes have not been checked in the live game yet
