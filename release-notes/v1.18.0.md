# Nrage Assistant — Raids in Groups (v1.18.0)

Groups can now put together raids, not only parties.

## ✨ New

### Groups
- Pick **Raid** as the type of a new group and choose its size: **10, 20, 25 or 40**. The form suggests the tanks,
  healers and DPS for that size, and you can change them with the + and - buttons
- The board shows a raid as **Raid 25** with how many members it has, and a new **Raids** filter
- Guildmates apply with a role as usual. When you invite and your party has two or more players, it becomes a raid
  first
- A raid post stays open while the raid grows and closes by itself when it reaches its size. You can also post a raid
  while you are already in one

## ⚠️ Good to know

- Raid posts only show for members on this version or newer. Older versions keep seeing parties
- Turning the party into a raid and the raid roster seats have not been checked in the live game yet
