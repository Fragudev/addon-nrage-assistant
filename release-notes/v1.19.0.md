# Nrage Assistant — Layers removed (v1.19.0)

Everything about layers is gone from the addon: tracking your layer, sharing it with the guild and the Layers tab.

## 🧹 Removed

### Layers
- The **Layers** tab and the layer part of **Home**
- The **Share my layer** switch: the addon no longer reads or sends your layer
- The layer line in the tooltip of map pins, and the grey border of members on another layer
- The `/nrage debug layer` command

## ⚠️ Good to know

- Your saved layer setting is deleted the first time you log in with this version. Nothing else is touched
- Guildmates on an older version may still send their layer; this version ignores it quietly
