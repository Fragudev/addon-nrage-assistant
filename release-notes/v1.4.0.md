# Nrage Assistant — Installed check (v1.4.0)

Guild masters and co-guild masters can now see who in the guild runs the addon, and which version.

## ✨ Features

### Installed check (Admin)
- New **Installed check** tab in the Admin group, for the GM and CoGM only
- Every guild member with a status: **Up to date**, **Outdated**, **Not installed**, **Never seen** (plus *Installed* when the version is not known yet and *Incompatible* for a version too old to sync)
- Shows each member's **version**, rank and when they were **last seen**; offline members keep the last version seen
- Filters: search by name, status chips with how many members each holds, a version dropdown and **Online only**
- **Ask online members** makes everyone online answer with their version again (at most once a minute)

## ⚠️ Good to know

- The list fills in as members log in after you update: at first many will show as *Never seen*
- Members on older versions are detected too, but only the ones updated to v1.4.0 answer *Ask online members*
- The new tab has not been checked in the live game yet
