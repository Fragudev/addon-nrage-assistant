# Nrage Assistant — Kill on Sight warning fix (v1.14.1)

The Kill on Sight warning no longer gets in the way of the game's own popups.

## 🐛 Fixes

### Intel
- The Kill on Sight warning no longer covers the **Release Spirit** button when you die: the game's popups now always
  stay on top of it and can be clicked
- The warning now starts lower on the screen, away from the popups. If you already dragged it somewhere, it stays there

## ⚠️ Good to know

- The warning can still be dragged anywhere with the left mouse button, and remembers where you put it
- This fix has not been checked in the live game yet
