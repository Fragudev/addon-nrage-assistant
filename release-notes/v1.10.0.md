# Nrage Assistant — Group alerts, sidebar scroll and profession names (v1.10.0)

New groups are announced at the top of the screen, the sidebar works in a short window, scrollbars can be dragged,
and profession names follow your language.

## ✨ New

### Groups
- When another member opens a new group, a short gold line appears at the top of the screen with its type, title and
  leader, for example "New group (Dungeon): Deadmines - Ann"
- No line for your own group, nor when a group is repeated, edited or closed
- The first two minutes after login stay quiet, so the groups that were already open do not all alert at once

## 🔧 Changes

### Window
- The sidebar scrolls when the window is too short for all its items; the user card stays at the bottom
- Every scrollbar can be dragged with the mouse, and a click on the track jumps there

## 🐛 Fixes

### Professions
- Profession names are shown in the language of the addon: an English client no longer shows "Cocina" because a
  guildmate with a Spanish client shared their recipes first
- Profession readiness rules match across languages: a rule made in Spanish ("Cocina 150") is met by a player whose
  client is in English ("Cooking"), and the other way round

## ⚠️ Good to know

- The group alert only reaches members who run this version or later
