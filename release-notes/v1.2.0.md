# Nrage Assistant — Groups (v1.2.0)

Groups is a board where guild members post a party for a dungeon or a quest. Others apply with a role, and the leader
invites them with a click.

## ✨ Features

### Groups
- New **Groups** tab in the Guild part of the window
- Post a party with a type, a title, the roles you need, and optionally a minimum level and a note
- Apply to an open post with the role you go as; you can withdraw, and an application expires if the leader does not answer
- The leader sees each applicant with **Invite** and **Decline**, and a popup when someone applies
- **Invite** sends the game invitation; nothing is invited for you
- The board can be filtered by type and by role, and each post shows how many of its five seats are taken
- Your post closes by itself when the party is full, when the group changes, or after an hour
- The sidebar shows how many groups are open

## ⚠️ Good to know

- Only members with the addon see the board, and everyone who wants to see or post a group needs this version: older versions ignore the messages
- Nothing is saved: a `/reload` clears your own post and your own application
- Joining a party may move you to the leader's layer, as a layer swap does; the addon does nothing about it
- A group and a layer swap cannot run at the same time
- Groups has not been tested in the live game yet
