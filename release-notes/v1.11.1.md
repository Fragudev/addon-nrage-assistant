# Nrage Assistant — Groups icon anywhere on the screen (v1.11.1)

The Groups icon no longer depends on the minimap, so it also shows with UIs that replace or reshape it.

## 🔧 Changed

### Groups
- The **Groups icon** now floats on the screen instead of sitting on the edge of the minimap. By default it appears
  near the top right of the screen
- Drag it anywhere; its position is remembered for the account and it cannot leave the screen
- Hover, the count of open groups and the click to open the Groups tab work as before

## ⚠️ Good to know

- The icon only shows while **another** member who runs the addon has an open group; your own group never shows it
- The position you gave it on the minimap in v1.11.0 is not carried over
