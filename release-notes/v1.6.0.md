# Nrage Assistant — Who is in the group (v1.6.0)

The Groups tab now shows who is already in each group: their class and, when it is known, their role.

## ✨ Features

### Class and role seats in Groups
- The five diamonds of each post are now five **seats**. A taken seat shows the **class icon** of that member, framed in the class color
- A small mark in the corner tells the **role**: shield for Tank, cross for Heal, swords for DPS
- A free seat shows the role the leader still needs, dimmed, so you can see at a glance that a group is missing a healer
- The tooltip lists the members as text ("3 of 5 members", then "Warrior · Tank"...)

## ⚠️ Good to know

- The leader's role is the one declared in their profile, and the role of a member who joined from the board is the role they applied as. A member who was in the party before the group was posted shows their class with no role mark
- Posts from players on an older version of the addon show people instead of classes until they update
- The seats have not been checked in the live game yet
