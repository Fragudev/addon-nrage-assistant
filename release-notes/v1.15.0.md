# Nrage Assistant — New Groups icon (v1.15.0)

The floating Groups icon has a new look, can be resized, and lets you know when someone opens a new group.

## ✨ New

### Groups
- The floating Groups icon now shows the **guild logo**, with a badge counting the open groups (1 to 9, then "9+").
  Your own groups are counted and listed too, but you cannot join them yourself
- **Right-click** the icon to open its settings: change its **size** and choose whether it is **always shown**, with
  Apply, Cancel and Reset buttons. The icon is always shown by default
- The "always show" option is also available in the **Groups** tab
- When another member opens a **new group**, the icon glows red and the battleground queue sound plays. The glow goes
  away when you open the Groups tab or hover the icon. No alert is shown if you are already looking at the Groups tab
- The queue sound also plays when your own group is published
- New command: `/nrage lfg icon show | hide | toggle` to control the icon visibility

## ⚠️ Good to know

- Groups that were already open when you log in do not trigger the glow
- The new icon and alerts have not been checked in the live game yet. If something looks wrong, tell an officer
