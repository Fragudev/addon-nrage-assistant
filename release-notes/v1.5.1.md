# Nrage Assistant — Rounder map buttons (v1.5.1)

The Guild and Enemies buttons on the world map are easier to see and to reach.

## 🎨 Changes

### World map buttons
- **Bigger**: 32 px instead of 22 px
- **Round**, with an opaque background and a ring, so they read well over any map
- Moved to the **middle of the right edge** of the map, Guild on top and Enemies below
- Lit (accent ring and icon) when on, dimmed when off; the tooltip now opens to the left, over the map

## ⚠️ Good to know

- The new look and position have not been checked in the live game yet
