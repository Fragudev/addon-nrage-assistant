# Nrage Assistant — First Beta (v0.1.0-beta)

Guild coordination and raid readiness for **Nrage** on World of Warcraft: Forever.
Open it with the minimap button or `/nrage`. Everything is done from the window.

> ⚠️ **Beta:** most features are covered by automated tests but are still being verified in game. Please report anything odd to the officers.

## ✨ Features

### Window & settings
- New window with a sidebar, character card and a sync status pill in the header
- Settings (gear icon): language (**Automatic / Español / English**), minimap button, blacklist alert sound
- Minimap button with a status tooltip (next event, readiness score, sync queue) and a right-click menu
- Windows remember their size and position

### Profile
- Character sheet with class, level, item level and professions, each value marked as *verified*, *declared* or *received*
- Pick your main and second spec from dropdowns
- Your sheet is shared with the guild automatically; your notes stay private

### Readiness
- Checklist with a clear score (e.g. `1/3 met, 2 unknown`). Unknown never counts as met
- Automatic checks for level, item level, professions, enchants and active buffs
- One set of rules for the whole guild, edited by the guild master in the **Rules** tab

### Raid events
- Create events, track attendance, and manage assignments per encounter
- Assignment templates, with suggestions that match the event
- Attendance and loot reports
- Export and import raid data as text

### Loot council
- **Nrage Loot** popup: answer with *Main spec / Off spec / Other / Pass* in one click
- **Nrage Loot Council** voting window for GM, Officers and Raid Leaders: sort, filter, vote, and confirm awards
- Open votes, plus the minutes of every decision and its reason

### PvP
- Honourable kills counted automatically (open world and battlegrounds, total and today)
- PvP rank with progress to the next one
- Guild ranking (Total / Today), shared automatically
- Recent kills feed

### Professions
- Search for who in the guild can craft a recipe, with online members listed first
- **Ask** button that drafts the whisper for you
- Recipes are shared automatically when you open your professions

### Blacklist
- A synced guild blacklist with reasons
- Alerts (chat line, raid warning and sound) when a listed player is in your group

### Layers
- Detects your current layer and shows the layers your guildmates have shared
- **Request swap**: guildmates on the target layer get a Help/Ignore popup and invite you

### Guild roles & sync
- The guild master is the only admin and assigns Raid Leader and Council roles and rosters
- Fully automatic sync. Changes queue up during encounters and are sent when the encounter ends

## 🔒 Access
The addon only works for characters in **Nrage** (Alliance, PvP ruleset). Shared data is accepted only from guild members.
