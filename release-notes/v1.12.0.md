# Nrage Assistant — Raid section, Notice board and same-zone layer swaps (v1.12.0)

Raid, Raid check and Loot now live together in one section, the announcements board has a new name, and layer swaps
follow the new layering rules of the game.

## ✨ New

### Raid section
- **Raid**, **Raid check** and **Loot** are now one **Raid** item in the sidebar, with three tabs at the top of the page
- The tabs follow your rank and roster as before: you only see the ones you can open, and the strip hides when there
  is only one
- The minimap menu groups them the same way

### Notice board
- The **Announcements** tabs are now called **Notice board** (**Tablón de anuncios** in Spanish)

## 🔧 Changed

### Layers
- The game now has separate layers for each zone, and an invite only moves you when you and the member who invites
  you are **in the same zone**. Layer swaps follow this:
  - Only guildmates **in your zone** are asked to help, and the layer list shows the layers of your current zone
  - Your layer is read again when you change zone
  - After joining the group, press **Refresh Now** when the game offers it (out of combat); the addon now waits up to
    5 minutes for the move
- Your zone is shared with your layer (only the zone, never your position in it), and only while **Share my layer**
  is on

## ⚠️ Good to know

- The new layer rules come from player reports, and nobody has tested them in game yet. If a swap does not work,
  tell an officer
- Members on an older version still answer swap requests. They can't tell which zone they are in, so the zone list
  leaves out their layers until they update
