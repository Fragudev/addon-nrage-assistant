# Nrage Assistant — Intel (v1.1.0)

Intel shows the enemy players around you in PvP, keeps a guild Kill on Sight list and turns kills into bounties.

## ✨ Features

### Intel
- New **Intel** sub-tab in PvP: who is nearby, the last hour, your Ignore list, the Kill on Sight lists and statistics
- Enemy players are seen through their nameplates in battlegrounds, arenas and world PvP, with their level, class, race and guild when the game shows them
- A compact window lists them: a left click targets, Shift marks Kill on Sight, Ctrl ignores, and a right click opens a menu
- Alerts for new enemies, Kill on Sight players, guild-listed players, stealth casts and the races you choose
- Your Kill on Sight list stays on your character; the **guild list** is shared, with a category and a reason for each player
- Sightings are shared with the guild, so everyone sees the enemies you meet
- Wins (honourable kills) and losses against each enemy, with `/nrage intel stats` and a report for the season
- **Bounties:** the GM and CoGM turn a guild-listed player into a PvP mission for kills of that player
- Import of Spy's Kill on Sight and Ignore lists, and the minimap button shows the nearby and Kill on Sight counts
- Commands `/nrage intel` and `/nrage spy`

## 🐛 Fixes

- Each crafter is listed once per recipe in the Professions tab

## ⚠️ Good to know

- Saved data moves to a new format: going back to an older version disables the addon
- Everyone should update: older versions ignore bounties and the guild Kill on Sight list
- Enemy nameplates must be on for Intel to see enemies, and a stealthed enemy is not seen
- Sharing sightings is on by default and can be turned off in the Intel settings; a sighting shows your position to the guild
- Intel is not tested in the live game yet: keep Spy installed until the officers confirm that Intel works
