# Nrage Assistant — Ranks & Rosters Beta (v0.1.0-beta.10)

The window is reorganised into **Guild** and **Admin**, and what you can do now follows your **guild rank**. Most
leaders work on their own roster.

> ⚠️ **Beta:** most features are covered by automated tests but are still being verified in game. Please report anything odd to the officers.

> 🔄 **Everyone should update.** Event sign-ups and the raid requirements of each roster only reach players on this
> version. Older versions keep working but miss them.

## ✨ Features

### Ranks
- Your permissions come from your guild rank: **GM, CoGM, Oficial, RL, Roster, Miembro, Alter**. Nobody assigns roles inside the addon any more
- A rank the addon does not recognise is treated as **Alter**
- The PvP officer flag is gone: PvP administration is for the GM and the CoGM

### Window
- Two groups in the sidebar: **Guild** (Home, Professions, Raid, Raid check, Loot, PvP) and **Admin** (Raid, Roster rules, Members, PvP)
- **Admin** is only shown to Oficial, RL and above
- Profile, Layers and Blacklist are hidden for now

### Home
- Your character at a glance: name, class, level and item level
- Pick your main and second spec
- Your layer, the guild's layers and layer swap requests, now on Home

### Raid
- **Guild → Raid:** the upcoming events of your roster and guild-wide events
- Answer **Going** or **Not going** with one click until the event starts
- **Admin → Raid:** events, attendance, assignments, templates and reports, as before, with everyone's sign-up next to their attendance
- Oficial and RL manage the events of their own roster; the GM and the CoGM pick any roster

### Raid check
- Your checklist against **your roster's** raid requirements (previously one list for the whole guild)
- For ranks Roster and above

### Loot
- Loot council per roster: the GM and the CoGM pick any roster, the rest see their own
- GM, CoGM, Oficial and RL open sessions, vote and award on their roster
- Ranks Roster and above put themselves forward from the **Nrage Loot** popup. Miembro and Alter no longer get it

### Admin
- **Roster rules:** the raid requirements of each roster. RL edits its own roster, Oficial reads them. Existing rosters keep the rules the guild had before
- **Members:** an RL adds and removes players in its own roster. Oficial reads; the GM and the CoGM manage every roster
- **PvP:** approvals, missions, season, prizes and deliveries, for the GM and the CoGM
