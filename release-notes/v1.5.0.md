# Nrage Assistant — World map toggles (v1.5.0)

Two buttons on the world map to show or hide the guild and the enemies, and a fix for big world PvP fights.

## ✨ Features

### World map toggles
- **Guild** button (people icon) at the top right of the world map: shows or hides your guildmates. It is the same setting as *Show guildmates on the world map*
- **Enemies** button (swords icon) next to it: shows or hides the enemies reported by Intel. Also available as *Show enemies on the world map* in the Maps group of the Intel settings, on by default
- Each button is lit when on and dimmed when off; the tooltip says what a click does
- Only the world map changes: the minimap pins and the Intel map mode stay as they were

## 🐛 Fixes

- **Automatic Intel announcements are capped at 3 per minute** across all enemies, on top of the once per enemy every 5 minutes rule. With dozens of enemies showing up at once, a player with announcements on could send dozens of chat lines in seconds, which can get them disconnected. Skipped enemies can still be announced later; *Only me* and manual announcements are not capped

## ⚠️ Good to know

- The world map buttons have not been checked in the live game yet
- The cap only matters to players who turned automatic announcements on (they are off by default). It is not confirmed to be the cause of the disconnections seen in big fights
