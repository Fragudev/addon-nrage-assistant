# Nrage Assistant — Kill points and fixes (v1.17.0)

Kills now earn PvP season points, your own kill ranking shows your real numbers again, and map pins no longer get
stuck on screen. The layer swap is gone.

## ✨ New

### PvP seasons
- Kills now give season points: **1 point for every world kill** and **1 point for every 5 battleground kills**.
  They add up with your mission points in the season ranking
- When a new season starts, kill points start again from zero. The points you earned stay in your all-time total
- Only kills made from this version on count. Earlier kills do not give points

### Professions
- Profession names now come from your game client when it uses the same language as the addon, and the addon's own
  list is used otherwise

## 🐛 Fixes

### PvP
- Some players saw **0** world and battleground kills for themselves in the kill ranking, while the rest of the guild
  saw their real numbers. Your own row now takes back the numbers the guild holds for you

### Map
- The diamond pins of guild members and enemies no longer stay in the middle of the screen after you close the map

## 🗑️ Removed

- The **layer swap** is gone, together with its popup. The Layers tab still shows your layer, the share switch and
  the layers of your guildmates

## ⚠️ Good to know

- Everyone needs this version for kill points to show up for every member in the season ranking
- Kill points, the map pin fix and the profession names from the client have not been checked in the live game yet
