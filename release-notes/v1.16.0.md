# Nrage Assistant — Duel missions and zone picker (v1.16.0)

PvP missions can now ask you to win duels, and officers can pick mission zones by continent or by zone.

## ✨ New

### PvP missions
- New mission type: **Duels won**. Only your own wins count, by knockout or because your opponent fled. A loss or
  a duel between other players nearby counts nothing
- Duel missions can be limited to some zones and to different opponents, and can have a daily cap per opponent
- New mission template: **Duelist: win 10 duels** (weekly, at most 3 wins against the same opponent per day)
- Duels never count for kill missions, and a duel against a player does not use up your daily kills of that player
- The zone field of the mission form is now a **zone picker**: **Whole world**, **Kalimdor**, **Eastern Kingdoms**,
  then every zone of each continent. Pick up to 5; click a selected zone to remove it
- A continent counts all its zones, and a zone also counts the cities inside it: for example, **Dun Morogh**
  includes **Ironforge** and **Elwynn Forest** includes **Stormwind City**

## ⚠️ Good to know

- Everyone needs this version to see duel missions. Members on an older version do not get them, and they count
  missions set to a continent or to a zone with a city in it wrongly. The mission form warns about this
- Every duel claim still needs the approval of an officer
- Duel detection and the zone ids have not been checked in the live game yet. If a duel or a kill is not counted,
  tell an officer
