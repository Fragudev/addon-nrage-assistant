# Nrage Assistant — Diamond map pins (v1.7.0)

Guildmates and enemies on the map no longer look like members of your party.

## 🎨 Changes

### Map pins
- **Diamond shape**: guild and enemy pins are now a class icon inside a diamond, so they stand apart from the round party and raid pins
- **Guild**: the diamond border keeps the class colour, and turns grey when the guildmate is on another layer
- **Enemies**: a red diamond with a **red swords badge** in the corner; the "!" for Kill on Sight players stays
- Slightly **bigger**: 15 px on the minimap and 18 px on the world map

## ⚠️ Good to know

- The new pins have not been checked in the live game yet
