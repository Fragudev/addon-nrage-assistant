# Nrage Assistant — Map pins with OpenMap (v1.17.1)

The diamond pins of guild members and enemies no longer get stuck in the middle of the screen when you use OpenMap.

## 🐛 Fixes

### Map
- With **OpenMap** installed, some diamond pins stayed in the middle of the screen after a fight or after closing the
  map. They now stay hidden until the map places them again
- Nothing changes if you do not use OpenMap

## ⚠️ Good to know

- This fix has not been checked in the live game yet. If a pin still gets stuck, tell an officer
